## Going live (found on 19 November 2026)

The redesign can't simply be deployed. The test copy, https://gaff-test.vercel.app, runs a different line of work that was never merged into `claude/gaff-mvp`, the branch the redesign was built from.

- **What is live:** `claude/epic-ride-0ha68s` (8 November 2026): the marketplace (its requirements start `M`), the providers work (`P`: the Claude adapter with usage and a budget, Stripe Identity), the place index of 100,310 places from OpenStreetMap, real email through Resend, and nine made-up homes seeded by the build. Two more branches sit on top of it and aren't live either: `claude/gallant-darwin-dpy9oz` (the search map on Google Maps) and `claude/serene-davinci-b00vrg` (sign-in, `AU`).
- **Why the two don't combine:** both lines start from the same commit and share migrations 0000 to 0020 only. Each then has its own 0021 to 0036, for different tables. The test copy's database holds the live line's. The redesign built its own marketplace, search, saved homes, accounts, sign-in, verified identity and reports on a different schema.
- **Decisions that disagree:** both lines number their decisions from Q27, with different questions. The live line chose Stripe Identity (its Q48, Q60) and Google Maps (its Q58); the redesign says no identity vendor is chosen (its Q48) and there is no map (its Q49).
- **This is how things stood on 19 November.** The switch below was made on 20 November 2026: the test copy now runs this branch, and the old line is kept only as an archive.

### One project (the founder's objective, 19 November 2026)

"One live project, with all of the changes we intended to build." The plan:

- **The redesign is the base.** It is the agreed design for every screen, the account model and the navigation, and the larger and more tested of the two.
- **Everything the live line has that the redesign lacks comes across**, fitted to the redesign's tables, components and rules. The live line's requirement IDs (`M`, `P`, `AU`) are kept.
- **Where the two disagree:** the founder's latest explicit decision wins (no passwords, Q30 of 14 November; agents' own licences aren't checked for the pilots, Q38). Where the redesign only had a stopgap or a recommendation waiting to be confirmed, the founder's earlier decision on the live line wins: Google Maps (its Q58), the place index (its Q57), Stripe Identity (its Q48), the Claude API on the test copy (its Q37), documents and not open banking (its Q49).
- **Then the test copy switches**, with its database reset, and one branch carries on.

Sean agreed the plan on 19 November 2026. The decisions are Q50 to Q54 in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md); Q55, how Gaff knows someone belongs to an agency, is banked as open.

## Known issues

- **The credit under the search suggestions can't be reached by keyboard:** found on 20 November 2026 while fixing a home card's link under the open list (below). "Places from OpenStreetMap" is a link inside the list of suggestions, and the list closes when the box loses focus. So Tab from the box with the list open goes to the link as it disappears: focus drops to the page, and the next Tab reaches Search. A pointer reaches the link; a keyboard can't. `PlaceSearch` ("Where") is built the same way. Not fixed here: the list should stay open while focus is inside it.
- **A home card's link under an open list (fixed, 20 November 2026):** `UX-SR-14` at 390px failed its accessibility check when `ux-sr-search.spec.ts` ran alone, and passed after other journeys. Nothing was wrong for a renter: under the open suggestions the card could still be pressed across its whole width. axe measures a link by its own box, and the card's reach was a pseudo-element it can't see, so it took the title's line of text for the target and failed it when the credit link lay across that line. That happens at one height of the list: with this journey's own homes, two places with homes; after other journeys, six, and the list is taller. The reach is now an element (`.reach` in `src/ui/HomeCard`), so axe reports that it can't tell, not a failure. Nothing looks or behaves differently. `ActionList` and the map's small card still reach with `::after`.
- **Interim sender:** the test copy can send everything from Resend's test address (`MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS=onboarding@resend.dev`). Resend delivers that only to the Resend account owner's inbox, so renters, landlords and other members get nothing until Gaff's own sending domain is verified and the setting is removed.
- **Focus on the map's pins (UX-DS-20, 20 November 2026):** the lister's pin on the stand-in map now shows the same focus ring as every other control; it used to change the colour of its edge and nothing else. The credit link in the place search's list is out of the Tab order: with suggestions open, Tab landed on it as the list closed, and focus was lost. The focus check (`expectFocusRings`) now fails when focus is lost part way down a page, and the component page's test waits for the pin and holds that it was checked. **Still open:** the search map's stand-in pins and clusters show focus by their edge alone, with no ring, and no focus check reaches them yet.
- **Dark theme:** new in A4. It passes WCAG AA for every pair the screens use, and the dashboard, a letting, Approvals and the booking screen have been checked in it, but the founders haven't reviewed it yet. (A4 fixed the old dark-theme problem with text on `forest`: text on `navy` now uses `nav-ink`, 9.01:1.)
