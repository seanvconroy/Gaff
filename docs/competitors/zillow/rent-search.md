# Zillow: rent search with suggestions

Captured 8 November 2026 by Sean. Desktop. Homes for rent, typing "florida". The screenshot was shared in a planning session and isn't stored here.

## Observations

- A left rail: Search, Updates, Favorites, Plan and Inbox. The top bar: Buy, Rent, Sell, Get a mortgage and Find an agent; the logo; Manage rentals, Advertise, Get help and "Sign in".
- The filter bar:
  - a search box with a clear × and a search icon, outlined while in use;
  - buttons "For rent ▾" (outlined in blue while set), "Price ▾", "Beds & baths ▾", "Property type ▾" and "Filters ▾" (with a sliders icon);
  - a primary "Save search".
- Suggestions mix kinds, each with its own icon:
  - a state ("FL");
  - searches as phrases ("Florida City FL 33035 homes", "… single-family homes");
  - schools and school districts;
  - cities (Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville, Miami).

  They come from Zillow's own index.
- The map is on the left and large, with "Schools ▾" and "Draw" buttons, a "Map ▾" type menu and zoom. It's Google Maps ("Map data ©2026 Google, INEGI").
- The results are on the right: "Rental Listings", then for no results "We could not find any matching results… Try changing your search". After that:
  - "Search tips": enter features and a location or school; decrease the number of filters; increase the scope of your search;
  - a "Save this search" link to get email alerts.
- A disclaimer says "Listing order may reflect relevance, quality, and paid promotion." Gaff's order is never for sale (Q30).
