# The test copy's photos

Photos for the made-up homes on the test copy (`src/server/dev/seed.ts`), so its listings look as they will when Gaff is live. Sean supplied the five originals on 8 November 2026, for the test copy only. Before anything public uses them, check Gaff may, or replace them.

Each original is here in up to three framings: `-full`, and `-left` and `-right` crops (78% of the width). Each made-up letter uses one framing, because Gaff's copied-photo check (M29) holds a listing whose photos match another letter's. The seed's tests check that no two letters' photos are within the check's distance.

| Photo                      | Shows                                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `living-room-and-kitchen`  | A living room opening onto the kitchen            |
| `living-room-tall-windows` | A living room with tall windows and a corner sofa |
| `bed-by-the-window`        | A bed and sofa by tall windows with a city view   |
| `studio`                   | A studio with its kitchen, sofa and bed           |
| `outside`                  | An apartment building with balconies              |

They were made with sharp: cropped, at most 1,600 pixels wide, JPEG quality 80, with no location or camera details.
